First-run Legal gate

The app records acceptance in ~/.hawaldar/settings.json (legalVersion, legalAcceptedAt). That record is not an engagement letter or legal advice. Decline quits.

HITL PoC policy

Hawaldar is not an exploit platform. Proof probes:

  • Need explicit in-app approval, one prompt per probe
  • Run in the contained browser/tool image against in-scope URLs only
  • Refuse DELETE, data-mutating SQL, long timing delays, and cookie/storage/network exfiltration
  • May create benign test records (for example a throwaway user) — point them at disposable/staging targets
